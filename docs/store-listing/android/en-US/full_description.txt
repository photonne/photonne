Photonne is the app for your photos and videos, stored on your own server instead of another company's cloud. Everything you expect from a great photo app (automatic backup, smart search, face recognition, memories), with your memories under your control.

ALBUMS AND FOLDERS, YOUR WAY
Most photo apps make you pick a single way to organize. Photonne lets you use both at the same time:
• Albums: group photos without moving them. Perfect if you'd rather not think about where each file lives.
• Folders: create, rename and move your photos between real folders, for those who like everything sorted their own way.
• The same photo can live in its folder and in as many albums as you like.
• Smart albums that fill themselves based on the conditions you choose: people, dates, folders…
• "To organize": an inbox that suggests what to sort and files your photos by year in a few taps.

AI THAT WORKS FOR YOU
• Search the way you talk: type "beach at sunset" or "dog in the snow" and find the photo.
• Face recognition: name the people in your photos and see all their pictures together.
• Search by objects, scenes and the text that appears in your photos: receipts, signs, documents.
• Automatic memories: trips, people and what happened on this day in past years.
• Analyze any photo with AI whenever you want, from its menu.
• All analysis runs on your server: your photos are never sent to outside services.

AUTOMATIC BACKUP
• Photos and videos from your phone back up on their own, even in the background.
• Choose which phone folders to back up, and whether to do it only on Wi-Fi or while charging.
• Free up space on your phone by deleting only what is already safe on the server.
• Keeps each photo's original date and location.

YOUR WHOLE LIBRARY IN ONE PLACE
• Timeline that zooms by year, month and day, with quick jumps to any date.
• Map with your photos grouped by place.
• Favorites, archive and a trash you can restore from.
• Motion photos, videos and formats such as HEIC.
• Download any photo in its original format or as JPG.

SHARING MADE EASY
• Albums shared with family or friends, with view or edit permissions.
• Public links with a password, expiry date and view limit.
• Request links: your guests upload their photos from a wedding or a trip to your album, no account needed.
• Share any photo with other apps from the system share menu.

TRULY PRIVATE
• Your photos live on your server: nobody else sees them, analyzes them or uses them for advertising.
• No ads and no tracking.
• Several accounts on one server: everyone at home gets their own library.

WHAT YOU NEED
Photonne connects to a Photonne server, which you can run at home (on a computer or a NAS) or get from someone who manages one. Photonne is open source; the installation guide is at github.com/photonne/photonne.

Available in English and Spanish, with light and dark mode.
